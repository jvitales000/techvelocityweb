Platform

What SignalTrace does

Six services on one record. Everything a longitudinal motor assessment needs, and nothing it does not.

Spiral assessment scoring

Spiral drawings scored against CRST by a multimodal model, with the reasoning shown alongside.

Appointment scheduling

Clinician availability, patient booking, reminders, and rescheduling on one calendar.

Longitudinal trend charts

Every session on one axis, per patient and across the cohort, with score distribution.

Patient communication

Session summaries and secure messages between visits, attached to the right record.

Records management

Observations and diagnostic reports written to FHIR R4, exportable as JSON or CSV.

Clinician profiles

Verified credentials and subspecialty, so patients reach the clinician who fits.

Trust and security

Privacy built into the architecture

Patient names and MRNs live only in a local Identity Vault. Every clinical record and every AI call references a surrogate token instead, so the scoring zone never learns who the patient is.

Identity Vault

Identifying details stay in a local store, kept apart from clinical data.

Surrogate tokens everywhere else

Analytics, exports, and model calls operate on tokens only.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

AES-256 storage, TLS 1.3 transport, single sign on with MFA for clinicians.

Every access is logged

An append only audit trail records who opened which record, and when.

Verified clinicians only

Credentials are checked before a profile becomes visible to patients.
